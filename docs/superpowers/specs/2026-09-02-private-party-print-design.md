# Nobar Private Party Print Set - Design Specification

## Scope

Create three independent print artifacts for Nobar's third-birthday private event:

1. A 40 x 90 cm portrait parking-direction sign.
2. A 50 x 70 cm portrait private-event sign.
3. A small A5 portrait party menu, printed double-sided.

Each artifact must be delivered as its own print-ready PDF. Editable SVG source and PNG previews will also be supplied separately. Nothing is combined into one printable sheet.

## Visual Direction

Use the light, decorative direction approved in the visual companion:

- Warm off-white paper ground and near-black typography.
- A restrained five-colour palette derived from Nobar's current third-birthday Instagram artwork: orange, violet, pink, sky blue, and fresh green.
- Clear, simple layout with generous open space.
- Decoration carries the personality: thin black rules, irregular circles, hand-drawn curves, Vietnamese tone-mark shapes, and one original musician illustration.
- Avoid dark photographic backgrounds, generic luxury-bar styling, neon effects, gradients, and crowded poster layouts.

The visual system references the current @nobardalat birthday posts without copying their artwork. The website contributes its disciplined typography, Vietnamese tone-mark motif, and brand language.

## Typography

Use `iCiel Novecento Sans` from the repository for large display copy and `Beau Sans` for supporting copy. Large headings may use a slight oblique treatment, but letterforms must remain easy to read from the intended distance.

## Decorative Musician Illustration

Use one original full-body character playing a trumpet as a recurring visual motif. The character uses loose black contours, flat imperfect colour fills, and subtle print grain. It appears large on the private-event sign, medium on the menu cover, and only as a small supporting crop on the parking sign so the arrow and instructions remain dominant.

## Parking Sign - 40 x 90 cm

Primary copy:

- `ĐỖ XE ĐỐI DIỆN ĐƯỜNG`
- `PARKING IS ACROSS THE STREET`

Use a dominant horizontal arrow pointing toward the actual opposite side of the street. The arrow will be supplied as a right-pointing version unless the printer/site team requests the mirrored version. The Nobar wordmark and address sit in a small footer.

## Private Event Sign - 50 x 70 cm

Primary copy:

- `QUÁN ĐANG CÓ TIỆC RIÊNG`
- `PRIVATE EVENT TONIGHT`
- `Hẹn gặp bạn vào một đêm khác nhé.`

The musician illustration is the visual focus, with the notice still readable before decorative detail. The sign includes `NOBAR TRÒN 3 TUỔI` and `08.09.2026` as event context.

## Production and Verification

- Add 3 mm bleed and a safe margin appropriate to each format.
- Keep all essential text vector-based in the PDF.
- Embed or outline fonts in final print outputs.
- Render every PDF to PNG and visually inspect hierarchy, clipping, spacing, image quality, accents, and bilingual copy.
- Validate physical page dimensions and confirm that the three outputs are separate files.
